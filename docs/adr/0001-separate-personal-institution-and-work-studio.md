# Separate Personal Institution from Work Studio

Personal Institution remains the private evidence and reflection layer; Work Studio remains the execution, decision, and delivery layer. When personal material is relevant to a Work Object, it enters only through a user-approved, redacted Evidence Bridge or stable reference, rather than copying personal-memory records into Work Studio. This preserves privacy and provenance while allowing lived evidence to inform real work.

## Considered options

- Share stable links or minimal summaries only — accepted.
- Copy personal-memory records into Work Objects — rejected because it blurs ownership, sensitivity, and retention boundaries.
- Keep the systems entirely isolated — rejected because it prevents grounded lived evidence from informing work.

## Consequences

Skills that retrieve personal material must obtain approval before creating an Evidence Bridge. Work Objects may cite the bridge and its provenance, but must not become a duplicate personal archive.
