## Intent

Determine which additional business-management skills would make Work Studio
comprehensive across strategy, market, revenue, customer, finance, people,
operations, risk, governance, and growth without duplicating its four existing
business skills or its lifecycle specialists.

## Success evidence

<!-- Checklist of observable outcomes that indicate completion. -->
- [x] Current business-skill coverage and material gaps are mapped.
- [x] Candidate skills are grounded in attributable primary-source evidence.
- [x] Each recommendation has a distinct decision frontier, inputs, outputs,
      authority boundary, and route to existing Work Studio skills.
- [x] Candidates are prioritized into coherent implementation waves.
- [x] The Work Studio MCP server validates the governing Work Object.


## Constraints and non-goals

**Constraints:**
- Preserve Work Studio's Work Object lifecycle, evidence model, consequence
  gates, and specialist routing.
- Prefer reusable management decisions over vendor-specific playbooks.
- Keep external communication, money movement, personnel action, legal
  commitments, and live-system mutation behind explicit scoped authority.

**Non-goals:**
- Implementing or installing the recommended skills in this research pass.
- Creating one generic business-management omnibus skill.
- Contacting customers, staff, vendors, regulators, or live business systems.
